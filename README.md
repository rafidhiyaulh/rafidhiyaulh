## Research

**Hybrid Random Forest–ARIMA for Daily PM₁₀ Forecasting in Jakarta**, IEEE FMLDS 2026, Kobe, Japan
- Random Forest on 15 engineered features plus ARIMA on its residuals, trained on 29,220 daily records from 5 monitoring stations (2010–2025).
- Cut RMSE by 22.7% over a tuned Random Forest (RMSE 7.12, R² 0.89) and beat XGBoost; tuned with Optuna, explained with SHAP. Undergraduate thesis, graded A.
- [Paper & thesis](https://github.com/rafidhiyaulh/tugas-akhir)
